GAZETTE C-7
Clause 7.3: Cadre Ratio Ratio 1:2:6

Every technical department must maintain 1 Professor and 2 Associate Professors per 6 Assistant Professors for approved UG intake.

Clause 4.1: Land Contiguity Min 7.5 Acres

Campus land must be continuous single plot, unencumbered by public highways or non-educational easement rights.

Clause 12.2: Fire Safety NOC Valid > 90 Days

State Directorate of Fire Services certificate must have minimum 90 calendar days validity remaining on scrutiny cutoff date.
