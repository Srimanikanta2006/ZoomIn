Upon comprehensive algorithmic and expert technical scrutiny of the statutory documentation, infrastructural compliance metrics, and faculty registers uploaded by your institution, the Scrutiny Committee has recorded the following critical non-compliances and deficiencies:

1. Non-Submission of Valid Fire & Life Safety Certificate (Statutory Clearance) APH Cl. 2.14.3

Finding Details: The Fire Safety NOC submitted during document submission was issued by the District Fire Officer on 13.03.2022 and lapsed on 12.03.2025. In accordance with Clause 2.14.3 of APH 2025-26, no technical institution shall be granted Extension of Approval without a live and unconditional Fire Safety Certificate for student-occupied premises.

Prescribed Regulatory Directive: Submit renewed valid Fire Safety NOC issued by Chief Fire Officer or authorized district revenue commissioner within the cure period without exception.

हिन्दी अनुवाद: संस्थान द्वारा प्रस्तुत अग्नि सुरक्षा अनापत्ति प्रमाण पत्र (NOC) दिनांक १२ मार्च २०२५ को समाप्त हो चुका है। वैध अनापत्ति प्रमाण पत्र प्रस्तुत किया जाना अनिवार्य है।
2. Academic Cadre Ratio Deficit in Department of Computer Science & Engineering APH Cl. 7.1 / Table 7(A)

Finding Details: For the sanctioned intake of 180 seats in CSE, the statutory cadre distribution requires a ratio of 1:2:6 (Cadre Requirement: 2 Professors, 4 Associate Professors, and 12 Assistant Professors). The scrutiny roster indicates zero (0) Professors and only one (1) Associate Professor on regular cadre rolls.

Prescribed Regulatory Directive: Furnish University Selection Committee minutes demonstrating recruitment initiation or appointment orders for regular Professor cadre in CSE.

हिन्दी अनुवाद: कंप्यूटर विज्ञान एवं अभियांत्रिकी विभाग में प्राध्यापक एवं सह-प्राध्यापक कैडर अनुपात विहित मानकों (१:२:६) से कम पाया गया है।
3. Absence of Competent Revenue Authority Seal on Land Use Conversion Certificate APH Appendix 4, Item 8

Finding Details: The document uploaded towards Institutional Land Use Certificate for Survey Khasra Nos. 412/1 and 412/2 does not show an authenticated, legible stamp/seal from the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) / Competent District Revenue Authority on Page 4.

Prescribed Regulatory Directive: Upload a high-resolution, certified true copy of the official land conversion order carrying the verified district seal and registration index.

हिन्दी अनुवाद: प्रस्तुत भू-उपयोग परिवर्तन प्रमाण पत्र पर सक्षम राजस्व अधिकारी की सुपाठ्य मुहर अनुपस्थित पाई गई है।
