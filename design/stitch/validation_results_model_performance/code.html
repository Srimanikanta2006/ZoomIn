What We Miss

Algorithmic Boundaries & Edge Blindspots
4 Known Traps

An empirical accounting of structural vulnerabilities where computational document analysis cannot substitute for physical forensic scrutiny.

01
Off-Registry Asset Relocation ("The Travelling Lab")

Machinery photographed on-site during initial verification, but immediately shuttled to sister campuses or affiliated polytechnics post-inspection. Physical serial number tags match invoice, but geofenced presence is fleeting.

02
Undigitized Sub-Divisional Stay Orders

Litigation or land disputes lodged in local Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) courts that lack electronic court (e-Courts) indexing. Digital land title searches show clear revenue records despite active local civil injunctions.

03
Synthetic Foreign Partner Credentials

High-fidelity PhD credential letters with authentic-looking watermarks from unaccredited overseas entities or shut-down research foundations outside Association of Indian Universities (AIU) mutual recognition databases.

04
Unpledged Endowment Liquidity Pledges

Joint fixed deposit receipts (FDRs) deposited in district cooperative banks where encumbrance liens are registered manually on paper vouchers rather than via Central Banking Solutions (CBS) webhooks.

notification_important

Mandatory Policy Directive: Filings flagging any of these 4 vectors are automatically routed to the Regional Director's docket for physical on-site verification before Letter of Approval (LoA) issuance.
